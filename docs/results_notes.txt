Mental Health Screening Analysis – Key Results
NHANES 2017–2018 (Adults 18+)

Dataset overview:
- Source: NHANES 2017–2018
- Analytic sample: Adults (age ≥ 18) with complete PHQ-9 data
- Final sample size: 5,068 individuals

Depression severity:
- Clinically significant depressive symptoms (PHQ-9 ≥ 10):
  - n = 459 (9.1% of sample)
- High-risk depressive symptoms (PHQ-9 ≥ 15):
  - n = 167 (3.3% of sample)

Treatment access proxy:
- Treatment defined as self-reported contact with a mental health professional
  in the past 12 months (HUQ090).
- This proxy captures access/contact, not treatment quality or duration.

Treatment gap findings:
- Among adults with PHQ-9 ≥ 10:
  - 314 out of 459 were not in treatment
  - 68.41% reported no mental health professional contact in the past year

- Among adults with PHQ-9 ≥ 15:
  - 104 out of 167 were not in treatment
  - 62.28% reported no mental health professional contact in the past year

Key interpretation:
- A majority of adults with clinically significant depressive symptoms are not
  receiving mental health care.
- Even among high-risk individuals, most reported no recent mental health
  professional contact.
- These findings highlight a substantial treatment access gap in the U.S.
  adult population.

Caveats:
- PHQ-9 is a screening tool, not a diagnostic instrument.
- HUQ090 reflects mental health professional contact, not medication use,
  diagnosis, or continuity of care.
- Cross-sectional survey data; causal conclusions cannot be drawn.
